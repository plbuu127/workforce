5. In **Company · Reviewer**, open the case's **Review** section, enter a concrete message asking for the signed contract and corrected dates, then **Request changes**. Return to **My work · Employee**, upload **Signed contract**, tick consent and **Submit & run AI**. Pause on the version comparison: the verified run resolved the signature while three date findings remained. Never replace a different live result with an expected count.
6. Upload **Updated stay record** as employee and **Corrected company details** as reviewer. Return as employee and run AI again with consent. These are updated fictional records, not a real stay extension. The verified run had zero internal document findings.
7. Save required employee preparation items with honest evidence/follow-up notes. In **Company · Reviewer → Review**, fill the combined company checks, confirm the transition record and originals, review the findings and English draft, and record any external follow-up resolution explicitly. Select **Approve review**. Do not attest to real verification of fictional data.
8. Show the employee's corresponding completed outcome. Open **History**, compare versions and verify the audit trail. Use **Download preparation summary** to show that external follow-ups remain visible. Expand official references and model execution evidence briefly.

Reviewers can also correct source-linked AI fields with **Save correction & recheck**, retaining the original reading and reason. Demonstrate this only if the live reading genuinely needs correction, or record a separate clearly labelled controlled-error exercise. Do not manufacture a model mistake and present it as live.

## English narration (approximately 200 words)

Changing employers in Korea can leave foreign employees unsure what to prepare, while companies compare information across scattered documents.

Workforce gives both sides a shared next step. This demonstration uses fictional documents and real NVIDIA models on Nebius.

I create a company workspace and a new employee case. The employee joins, describes their situation, and sees what they should prepare and what the company must check.

Next, both sides upload their documents. The vision model reads the actual pages. Workforce compares the extracted facts and highlights missing signatures and conflicting dates. Each finding points back to the original evidence.

The reviewer requests changes. The employee replaces the unsigned contract and runs a new analysis. The signature finding resolves, while unrelated date issues remain.

After the remaining records are corrected, the reviewer checks the originals, records company evidence and external follow-ups, and approves that version of the preparation review.

The employee sees the same outcome. Version history, official references, model receipts and the audit trail make the process traceable. Reviewers can also correct an AI reading while preserving its original value and the reason for the correction.

Workforce helps employees and companies prepare together. It does not grant immigration approval or permission to work.
