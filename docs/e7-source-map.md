# Focused E-7-1 preparation: source map

Reviewed 2026-10-29 (Korea time). This is a bounded preparation workflow for a person already in Korea, reporting E-7-1, who is changing employers. It does not determine permission, eligibility, the correct occupation code or a filing deadline. Other situations retain general preparation support.

## Sources inspected

- [Immigration Act, Article 21](https://www.law.go.kr/lsLinkCommonInfo.do?chrClsCd=010202&lsJoLnkSeq=1031822861), effective 2026-10-02: prior permission is the default; designated professional exceptions report within 15 days. The application does not apply this exception automatically to E-7-1.
- [Enforcement Decree, Article 26-2](https://law.go.kr/lsLinkCommonInfo.do?chrClsCd=010202&lsJoLnkSeq=1031815259), effective 2026-10-02: designated qualifications and notification conditions apply.
- [Enforcement Rule, Article 76](https://law.go.kr/lsLinkCommonInfo.do?lsJoLnkSeq=1031814935), effective 2026-10-23: Annex 5-2 attachments and administrative information sharing.
- [Current rule page](https://www.law.go.kr/LSW/lsInfoP.do?lsiSeq=291289&efYd=20261023&ancYnChk=0) links to [Annex 5-2 PDF](https://www.law.go.kr/LSW/flDownload.do?flSeq=170812943&bylClsCd=110201). This annex itself is amended 2024-12-24. The common requirements and E-7 workplace-change row on pages 16–17 were extracted and visually inspected. The older indexed PDF was not used as the current attachment.
- [MOJ Notice 2020-212](https://www.law.go.kr/LSW/admRulInfoP.do?admRulSeq=2100000190656&chrClsCd=010201): registration, new-role qualifications, excluded occupations and departure circumstances constrain reporting. The app requires a reviewer to verify current occupation classification and the individual procedure rather than encoding a potentially outdated occupation whitelist.

## Implemented mapping

| Situation / requirement | Employee action | Company action | Basis |
|---|---|---|---|
| Passport / residence card | Keep originals ready; do not upload unnecessary identifiers | Check official-process readiness | Annex common row |
| Contract | Upload actual contract for extraction and comparison | Compare role, employer and start with company joining details | Annex E-7 row + internal comparison |
| Worked through contract end / mutually agreed end / workplace closure or suspension | Keep factual transition evidence for review | Confirm the reported exception actually applies | Annex E-7 consent exceptions |
| Early departure / another circumstance | Prepare previous-employer consent or record need for help | Resolve the applicable procedure; do not assume reporting eligibility | Annex + notice |
| Business / corporate registry | See company-owned responsibility | Record evidence or the information-sharing arrangement to verify | Annex E-7 row |
| Employment recommendation or necessity evidence | See company-owned responsibility | Prepare the applicable evidence for the exact occupation | Annex E-7 row |
| Occupation and route / conditional guarantee | Report registration and changed duties accurately | Record actual code, qualifications, permission/report route and any guarantee requirement | Article 21, notice, Annex |

The company joining-details upload is an internal comparison document, not a universal government requirement. Transition evidence is requested internally to support a claimed factual exception; the application does not assert a new statutory form. The general annex permits additional or omitted attachments in individual review and administrative information sharing. School-enrolment and other case-specific common requirements are outside this focused workflow and must be confirmed for an applicable individual case.

## Version and decision behavior

Preparation records have a responsible role, status, note and timestamp. An employee cannot attest company records, or vice versa. Unknown answers remain incomplete. Profile changes clear preparation dispositions; an analyzed-document change starts a new version and invalidates previous review and dispositions. Prior records remain in history. Pending preparation items block review completion. A follow-up may remain explicitly recorded, but then a reviewer resolution is mandatory and the completed page and downloaded summary retain the outstanding action. Completion is never presented as permission to work.
